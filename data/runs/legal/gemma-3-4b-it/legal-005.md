Alder Ventures is investing SEK 40,000,000 in Skarv Robotics AB for Series A Preferred Shares, increasing the post-money valuation to SEK 200,000,000 (fully diluted after a 20% option pool).  The investment gives Alder an estimated 37.5% ownership stake following dilution. They receive a 1x liquidation preference, non-participating and senior to Ordinary Shares. An expansive weighted average anti-dilution mechanism protects their equity. Conversion is 1:1 with adjustments possible.

The board will consist of two founder appointments, two from Alder, and one independent director. Protective provisions require Series A majority consent for key decisions.  Alder retains pro-rata rights in future rounds and a drag-along right if 60% of shareholders agree. Founder shares vest over four years with a one-year cliff, receiving 25% of time served. The term sheet is non-binding except for confidentiality and exclusivity for 45 days under Swedish law.
