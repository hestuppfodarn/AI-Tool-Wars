Here’s a plain English version of Clause 9 for shoppers:

**Returns and Refunds**

If you don't want the items we sent you, you can cancel your order within 14 days of receiving them. Just tell us in writing! You’ll need to send the items back to us at your own cost within another 14 days. We’ll then give you all the money you paid, including delivery charges, within 30 days once we receive the returned items.

If you've used and damaged the item before returning it, you might not get a full refund. You are responsible for protecting the goods while they are in your possession.
