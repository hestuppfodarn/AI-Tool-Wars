 The term sheet for Skarv Robotics AB's Series A investment by Alder Ventures Fund III is as follows:

- Investment: SEK 40,000,000 for Series A Preferred Shares.
- Post-money valuation: SEK 160,000,000, including a 20% option pool.
- Alder's resulting ownership percentage is not explicitly stated but can be calculated based on the investment and post-money valuation.
- Liquidation preference: 1x original purchase price, non-participating, senior to Ordinary Shares.
- Anti-dilution: broad-based weighted average.
- Option pool: 20% of the post-money fully diluted share capital.
- Board composition: five directors - two by Founders, two by Alder, one independent appointed by mutual agreement.
- Protective provisions: consent of Series A majority required for certain actions, including new senior securities, sale of the company, changes to the articles, and debt above SEK 5,000,000.
- Pro-rata rights: Alder may participate in future rounds to maintain its percentage.
- Drag-along: holders of 60% of shares can require all holders to sell.
- Founder vesting: 4 years, 1-year cliff, 25% credited for time served.
- Exclusivity: 45 days from signature.
- Governing law: Sweden.
