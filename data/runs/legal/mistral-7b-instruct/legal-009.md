 If you change your mind about the items you bought within 14 days of receiving them, you can return them to us for a full refund. Here's how it works:

1. Notify us in writing that you want to cancel the order within 14 days of getting the goods.
2. Send the items back to us at your own expense within another 14 days after notifying us.
3. We will refund all the money you paid, including shipping costs, within 30 days of receiving the returned items.

Please note that if you've used or damaged the goods beyond what's necessary to check them, we might not be able to give you a full refund.
