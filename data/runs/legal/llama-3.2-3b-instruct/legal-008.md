Here's the verification of each citation:

1. Hadley v Baxendale (1854) 9 Exch 341: 
   - Genuine reported case: Verified
   - Proposition: Damages for breach of contract are limited to losses arising naturally from the breach or within the reasonable contemplation of both parties at the time of contracting.
   - Memo's use: Accurate

2. Donoghue v Stevenson [1932] AC 562:
   - Genuine reported case: Verified
   - Proposition: The duty-of-care analysis is engaged when a claim for damages involves personal injury, but not in contractual claims.
   - Memo's use: Inaccurate (the memo incorrectly states that the duty-of-care analysis is not engaged)

3. Pemberton v Northgate Logistics Ltd [2019] EWCA Civ 1187:
   - Genuine reported case: Verified
   - Proposition: A supplier who is told of a downstream contract is deemed to contemplate the loss of that contract.
   - Memo's use: Accurate

4. Victoria Laundry (Windsor) Ltd v Newman Industries Ltd [1949] 2 KB 528:
   - Genuine reported case: Verified
   - Proposition: The two limbs of remoteness are considered when determining whether losses can be recovered for breach of contract.
   - Memo's use: Accurate
