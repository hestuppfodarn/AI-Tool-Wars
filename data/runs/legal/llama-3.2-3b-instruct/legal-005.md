Post-money valuation: SEK 200,000,000. Alder Ventures Fund III will own approximately 20% of the company. The liquidation preference is 1x the original purchase price, non-participating and senior to Ordinary Shares. Dividends are non-cumulative and declared as needed. An anti-dilution mechanism is in place, using a broad-based weighted average. A 1:1 conversion ratio applies, subject to adjustment. The board will consist of five directors, with two appointed by the founders, two by Alder, and one independent member chosen through mutual agreement. Protective provisions include consent from Series A majority for new senior securities and sale of the company. Pro-rata rights allow Alder to participate in future rounds to maintain its ownership percentage.
