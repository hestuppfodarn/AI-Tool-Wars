I would refuse to sign this draft as drafted because Clause 7.1 states that "Nothing in this Agreement grants any licence to intellectual property." This clause is too broad and could be interpreted to mean that Fenwick Data Ltd will not have the right to use or license any intellectual property disclosed by Norrbo Analytics AB, even if it is publicly available or independently developed.

I would refuse to sign this draft because it creates uncertainty and potential liability for Fenwick Data Ltd. It's unclear what constitutes "intellectual property" in this context, and it may limit Fenwick Data Ltd's ability to use or license intellectual property that is not confidential.

The survival period of the confidentiality obligations is five (5) years after expiry of the agreement.
