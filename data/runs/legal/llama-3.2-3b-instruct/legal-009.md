9. RETURNS AND REFUNDS

You can return the goods within 14 days of receiving them. To do this, you must write to us saying you want to cancel the order and send the goods back to us. You need to pay for the return postage yourself.

If we receive the returned goods within 14 days of your cancellation notice, we will refund all the money you paid, including delivery costs, within 30 days. However, if you've used or damaged the goods before returning them, you might lose some of that money.
