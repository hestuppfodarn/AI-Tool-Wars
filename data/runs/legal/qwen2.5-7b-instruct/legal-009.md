9. RETURNS AND REFUNDS

9.1 You can cancel the order within 14 days of receiving the goods without giving a reason. Just let us know in writing that you want to cancel.

9.2 If you cancel, return the goods to us at your expense within 14 days after telling us. We will refund all payments you made, including delivery costs, within 30 days of getting your returned items.

9.3 You must make sure not to use or handle the goods more than necessary to check them out. If their value goes down because of how you used them, you agree to pay for any loss in value.
