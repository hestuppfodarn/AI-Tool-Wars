1. **Article 28(3)(a) GDPR**: The DPA does not specify the data protection officer (DPO). This is a mandatory requirement under Article 28(3)(a).
   
2. **Article 28(3)(b) GDPR**: Clause 4 only mentions documented instructions for transfers to third countries, but it does not address other types of instructions from the Controller, such as those related to data processing activities or security measures.
   
3. **Article 28(3)(c) GDPR**: The DPA lacks a description of the technical and organizational measures taken by the Processor in accordance with Article 32 GDPR. This is a mandatory requirement under Article 28(3)(c).
   
4. **Article 28(3)(d) GDPR**: Clause 6 only mentions that appropriate technical and organisational measures will be implemented, but it does not provide details on these measures as required by Article 28(3)(d). Annex 2 should include more specific information.
   
5. **Article 28(3)(e) GDPR**: The DPA does not specify the criteria for assessing whether a sub-processor can ensure an adequate level of data protection, which is mandatory under Article 28(3)(e).
   
6. **Article 28(3)(f) GDPR**: Clause 8 only states that the Processor may engage sub-processors at its discretion without specifying any conditions or criteria for such engagement.
   
7. **Article 28(3)(g) GDPR**: The DPA does not include a description of the data security incident response plan, which is required under Article 28(3)(g).
   
8. **Clause 8 gets wrong**:
   - It fails to specify any conditions or criteria for engaging sub-processors.
   - It lacks oversight and control mechanisms that should be in place when engaging sub-processors, such as prior assessment of the sub-processor’s data protection capabilities.

In summary, the DPA is missing several key elements required by Article 28(3) GDPR, particularly regarding the description of technical and organizational measures, criteria for assessing sub-processors, and a security incident response plan. Additionally, Clause 8 does not provide sufficient oversight or control mechanisms when engaging sub-processors.
