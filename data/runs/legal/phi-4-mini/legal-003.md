Article 28(3) GDPR requirements missing or defective in the DPA:

1. **Purpose limitation**: The processing activities must be for specified, explicit and legitimate purposes (Art. 5(1)(a)). Clause 2 does not specify that data will only be processed to host/apply patient-booking functions.
   - Missing/Defective clause: Purpose of storage, backup & transmission.

2. **Data minimisation**: The processing should concern the minimal amount necessary for achieving its purpose (Art. 5(1)(c)). Clause 3 lists more data than may be strictly required; appointment times and types could potentially limit this.
   - Missing/Defective clause: Categories of personal data collected.

3. **Storage limitation period**: Personal data should not kept longer than necessary for the purposes it was processed (Art. 5(1)(e)). Clause 6 does not mention any storage limitations or deletion policies after a certain time frame, which could lead to indefinite retention.
   - Missing/Defective clause: Storage duration and destruction policy.

4. **Transparency**: The Controller must provide information about the processing activities in their privacy notice (Art. 12 GDPR). Clause 1 does not mention any transparency obligations or how data subjects will be informed of these practices, which is a key requirement under Art. 13.
   - Missing/Defective clause: Transparency and informing individuals.

5. **Data subject rights**: The Controller must inform the individual about their right to access (Art. 12 GDPR). Clause 4 does not mention any specific obligations regarding data subjects' rights, such as accessing or rectifying personal information they hold.
   - Missing/Defective clause: Data Subject Rights Information.

6. **Data Protection Impact Assessment**: For high-risk processing activities that may result in a risk to the fundamental rights and freedoms of individuals (Art. 35 GDPR), an assessment must be conducted prior to data processing, which is not mentioned anywhere within this DPA.
   - Missing/Defective clause: Data protection impact assessments.

7. **Data Protection Officer**: If required by certain conditions such as systematic monitoring or large-scale profiling activities that could result in a high risk for individuals (Art. 37 GDPR), the Controller must appoint an independent and expert data protection officer, which is not mentioned within this DPA.
   - Missing/Defective clause: Data Protection Officer.

8. **Security measures**: The Processor should implement appropriate technical and organisational security measures to ensure personal data's integrity and confidentiality (Art. 32 GDPR). Clause 6 mentions implementing "appropriate" but does not detail the specific requirements or standards that must be met.
   - Missing/Defective clause: Specific Security Measures.

9. **Sub-processors**: The Processor should obtain explicit consent from individuals before engaging sub-processors unless it's necessary for a contract (Art. 28 GDPR). Clause 8 allows engagement of "sub-processors at its discretion," which lacks the requirement to inform data subjects or seek their permission.
   - Missing/Defective clause: Sub-processor Consent.

10. **Data breach notification**: The Processor must notify competent supervisory authorities within 72 hours after becoming aware (Art. 33 GDPR). Clause 7 mentions a delay of up to 48 hours for notifying the Controller, which is not compliant with Art. 33.
    - Missing/Defective clause: Notification time frame.
